# Safety and ethics

This project is for materials intelligence, not procurement advice or safety certification.

## Powder safety

Metal and polymer powders can create inhalation, fire, explosion, and contamination risks. Reactive powders such as titanium and aluminum require special handling. Always follow SDS, local rules, and lab procedures.

## Resin safety

Uncured resins can irritate or sensitize skin and eyes. Track PPE, ventilation, washing, curing, and waste disposal.

## Price ethics

Do not publish confidential supplier quotes or private documents. Store only derived observations you are allowed to share. Mark manual/private data clearly.
