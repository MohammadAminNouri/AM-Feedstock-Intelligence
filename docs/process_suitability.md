# Process suitability guide

## Metal powder

- LPBF: fine spherical powder, often 15-53 µm, strong flow and chemistry control.
- EBM: coarser conductive powder, often titanium/CoCr, vacuum/preheat behavior matters.
- DED: coarser and broader PSD tolerated, catch efficiency matters.
- Binder Jetting: packing density and sinterability dominate more than laser absorptivity.

## Polymer powder

- SLS: thermal window, refresh ratio, powder flow, and aging dominate.
- MJF: powder age/reuse and fusing-agent compatibility are critical.

## Filament

- PLA/PETG: easy, low barrier.
- ASA/ABS: enclosure and ventilation become important.
- Nylon/PC: drying becomes critical.
- PEEK/PEKK/PPS/PEI: high-temperature printer required.
- CF/GF-filled: hardened nozzle and abrasion tracking required.

## Resin

- Compatibility is not only material name. It depends on wavelength, exposure profile, printer ecosystem, post-cure, and safety certification.
