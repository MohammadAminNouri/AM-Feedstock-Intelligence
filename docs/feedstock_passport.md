# Feedstock passport

A feedstock passport is a compact quality card for each AM material.

## Universal passport fields

- feedstock class: powder, filament, resin, pellet, wire, slurry
- material family and grade
- product name
- supplier and true manufacturer
- supplier type
- process compatibility
- package size and price observation
- source URL and access date
- price confidence
- qualification level
- handling/safety risks
- sustainability or reuse signal

## Powder-specific passport fields

- PSD range and distribution method
- morphology/sphericity
- atomization or production route
- apparent density, tap density, Hall/Carney flow
- oxygen/nitrogen/hydrogen for reactive metals
- reuse/refresh rule
- satellite/fines/spatter contamination risk

## Filament-specific passport fields

- diameter and tolerance
- spool/net weight
- drying condition
- nozzle/bed/chamber range
- abrasiveness
- warp/shrinkage risk

## Resin-specific passport fields

- bottle volume
- wavelength/profile compatibility
- viscosity
- shelf life/open-bottle age
- post-cure condition
- uncured resin hazard
